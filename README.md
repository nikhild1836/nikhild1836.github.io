The other is a fun/hobby android app:

[Chronoquiz](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.github.nikhild1836.chronoquiz)
ChronoQuiz is a lightweight, text-only timeline history game where you can combine your topics of interest from a list of more than 40 topics like Political events, law, finance, education, sciences, art, music, dance, agriculture, food, handguns, movies, TV shows, soccer, cricket and more! Billions of possible combinations. Share customized quiz of the week with your friends/groups. Five instant topics for conversation!
